Apple vs Google maps

The UX on navigation is better for Apple Maps on iPhone. Google Maps just never got that smooth in its turn-by-turn navigation on CarPlay. It has on Android Auto. Something probably 99.9% of people wouldn't care about, but somehow it bothers me.

Am willing to sacrifice real-time traffic data and times for the UX. So much so that I risked using Apple Maps on a near 1,500 km trip, and somehow did end up using Google Maps for 5% of the time. In India. That's a decent win for Apple Maps.

Of course, Apple Maps is way off when it comes to listed places.
