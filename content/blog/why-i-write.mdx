I started this blog not because I had something profound to say, but because I wanted to learn more effectively.

## Writing as Thinking

There's a quote often attributed to Flannery O'Connor: *"I write because I don't know what I think until I read what I say."* That captures it perfectly.

When I try to explain something in writing, the gaps in my understanding become painfully obvious. That's the point. Writing forces clarity.

## The Compound Effect

Every post I write becomes a reference I can come back to. Six months from now, when I've forgotten the details of how something works, I'll have my own explanation waiting — written in my own words, at my own level of understanding.

## Getting Started

If you've been thinking about starting a blog, here's my advice: **just start**. Don't worry about having an audience. Don't worry about being original. Write for yourself first.

The best time to start writing was a year ago. The second best time is today.
